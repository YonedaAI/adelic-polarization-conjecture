## Attribution findings

The external literature entries resolve. The initial snapshot cited CCMar, Section 4.1, equations (4.3) to (4.6), at line 965. Those equations occur in Section 4 before subsection 4.1, Cyclic module. The manuscript author corrected the locator to Section 4, equations (4.3) to (4.6), and the correction was rechecked. Equation (4.6) supports the actual inverse-action convention used in the manuscript.

The Sonin statements have the correct strength. CCM sections 4.5 to 4.8 supply the shell generator, transition multiplier, mixed pairing, and changing norms. Theorem 4.6 proves a bounded isomorphism with bounded inverse; the statement after equation (62) explicitly allows different inherited inner products. The paper does not attribute isometry to that theorem.

The explicit formula matches CC2020 Appendix B, equations (147) to (150), after the stated centered Mellin substitution. Appendix C, Proposition 1, equation (155), supports the RH criterion on compact smooth multiplicative tests with Mellin transform vanishing on a finite set disjoint from the nontrivial zeros and containing 0 and 1. Choosing that set as exactly {0,1} gives the manuscript's two pole moments and sign convention. There is no restriction to even or real tests in the source criterion.

The sheaf claim matches Connes2026 section 7.3, Theorem 7.2 and its following Hochschild-homology sentence. CCMar's constructed trace pairing is kept distinct from the unproved arithmetic sign. The manuscript makes no universal priority claim for the elementary obstructions.
