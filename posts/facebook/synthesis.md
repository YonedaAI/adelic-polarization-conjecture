A nonzero class can disappear when one more prime is added.

Adelic Polarization Conjecture constructs a relative homology class detected at the origin. For its compact-bump representative, adding any new prime kills the class. The proof writes down a chain whose boundary is the image of the original class, including at the prime 2.

This is a specific test of the proposed arithmetic construction. A class that disappears cannot become a surviving class in its direct limit. Other classes could behave differently; their survival is still undetermined.

The 21-page synthesis defines the restriction cone and its commuting prime maps. Turning those objects into an arithmetic polarization still requires geometric degree maps and an independently constructed pairing with the required sign. That pairing remains unconstructed.
